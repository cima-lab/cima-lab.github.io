People

The people behind the lab

CIMA Lab is an emerging, cutting-edge research group that brings together two principal investigators from the School of Engineering, Applied Science and Technology, affiliated faculty from elsewhere in Canadian University Dubai, and the graduate and undergraduate researchers who do the day-to-day work. Partner institutions in the UAE and abroad round out the group.

Leadership 2 people

Dr. Haythem El-Messiry

Supervisor and Principal Investigator

Associate Professor, Department of Computer ScienceSEAST, Canadian University DubaiPhD, University of Ulm

Haythem El-Messiry is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Canadian University Dubai, working across computer vision, deep learning for medical image analysis and volume electron microscopy. He completed his PhD in computer vision at the University of Ulm. He is principal investigator on the Dubai RDI grant "Towards high-resolution 3D reconstruction in volume electron microscopy".

Computer visionVolume electron microscopyMedical image analysis

Dr. Firuz Kamalov

Supervisor and Principal Investigator

Professor of Mathematics and Machine LearningSEAST, Canadian University DubaiPhD, University of Nebraska

Firuz Kamalov has been a faculty member at Canadian University Dubai since 2011, working across machine learning, time series analysis and medical AI. He is Editor-in-Chief of the Gulf Journal of Mathematics and a Stanford-Elsevier top 2% cited scientist. He is principal investigator on the Dubai RDI grant "Identifying prodromal and preclinical markers for predicting diagnosis and progression in dementia".

Machine learningClinical predictionNeuroimaging

Affiliated faculty 1 person

Dr. Mohammad Nami

Affiliated Faculty

Associate Professor and Chair, Cognitive Neuroscience and Clinical NeuropsychologySchool of Health Sciences, Digital Innovation and Sustainability, CUDPhD, Institute for Cognitive Science Studies

Mohammad Nami works in medical and cognitive neuroscience, clinical neuropsychology and the neuroscience of sleep, and directs the Brain, Cognition and Behaviour Unit at BrainHub UAE. As affiliated faculty he connects the lab's imaging and clinical prediction work to cognitive neuroscience.

Graduate researchers 2 people

Tahamid Hossain

Graduate Research Assistant

Supervisor: Dr. Haythem El-MessiryCanadian University Dubai

Tahamid Hossain works on Microsam_llrd, a LoRA and layer-wise learning-rate-decay recipe for fine-tuning MicroSAM on mitochondria instance segmentation in volume electron microscopy. He also handles evaluation and release engineering for the lab's code, and maintains the CIMA Lab GitHub organisation and this website.

MicroSAMLoRA fine-tuningInstance segmentation

Junaid Aziz
