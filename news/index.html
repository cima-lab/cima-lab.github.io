CIMA Lab launches its public home

September 2026

CIMA Lab (Computational Imaging and Medical AI) is now live as a research lab of the School of Engineering, Applied Science and Technology (SEAST) at Canadian University Dubai. Our work is organised in the open under the cima-lab GitHub organisation, which currently hosts four repositories covering electron-microscopy segmentation, stitching and inpainting tooling. The lab's early work is supported by two Dubai RDI grants.

SEAST hosts a workshop on AI in medical imaging and diagnostic applications

Talks on generative modelling, explainable AI and 3D medical image segmentation at the CUD Incubator, with participants from MBZUAI and the CUD MSc in AI. Dr. Hanan Hussein presented the lab's segmentation work.

Jul 2025 · Funding

Canadian University Dubai to lead three DFF funded projects to advance healthcare innovation

The Dubai Future Foundation RDI programme awards three grants to CUD. Two of them, on explainable AI for dementia and on volume electron microscopy, are the programmes CIMA Lab now carries out.
