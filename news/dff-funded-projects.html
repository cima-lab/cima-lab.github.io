Canadian University Dubai to lead three DFF funded projects to advance healthcare innovation

The Dubai Future Foundation Research, Development and Innovation programme awarded three grants to Canadian University Dubai. Two of them, led by Dr. Firuz Kamalov and Dr. Haythem El-Messiry, are the programmes that CIMA Lab now carries out.

18 July 2025Canadian University DubaiSource: CUD News

Researchers at Canadian University Dubai were awarded three grants from the Dubai Future Foundation Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) programme to apply new technology to health in the UAE. CUD faculty lead each project.

Two of the awards went to the School of Engineering, Applied Science and Technology. Dr. Firuz Kamalov, working with the Abu Dhabi School of Management and the University of Huddersfield, leads a project to build an explainable artificial intelligence system for the early detection of dementia and for understanding how it may progress. Earlier diagnosis can improve treatment, guide clinical decisions and give families time to prepare.

The second SEAST award is led by Dr. Haythem El-Messiry with partners at Shandong University and Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence. It focuses on volume electron microscopy, a family of imaging techniques that reveal the 3D ultrastructure of tissue at nanometre resolution. The project is building a fast, robust and high-precision image processing workflow for large-scale 3D structure analysis, closing the gap between cell biology and tissue biology.

A third award, in the School of Health Sciences and Psychology, is led by Dr. Mohammed AlKhaldi with Dr. Wissam Ghash and collaborators at McGill University and the University of Bonn. It will set up a Health Technology Assessment system for the UAE, combining AI and web platforms to support evidence-based decisions on the value and reimbursement of health technologies.

CUD Provost Dr. Adam Fenech said the grants recognise the university's role in health research and innovation in the UAE and the wider region, and that with the support of the Dubai Future Foundation and institutional partners the projects could set a benchmark for innovation with global impact.

These two SEAST projects are the research programmes behind CIMA Lab. The grant records, code repositories and related publications are on the research page.
