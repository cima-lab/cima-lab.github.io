Computational Imaging and Medical AI

From 3D microscopy to early dementia diagnosis.

CIMA Lab is built on two funded research programmes at Canadian University Dubai: high-resolution 3D reconstruction in volume electron microscopy, and an explainable AI system for early detection of prodromal Alzheimer's disease from cognitive, behavioral and biomarker data.

A research lab of Canadian University Dubai

CIMA Lab is a research lab of the School of Engineering, Applied Science and Technology at Canadian University Dubai. The university's MSc in Artificial Intelligence supplies the graduate researchers who carry our projects, alongside undergraduate research assistants from the same school. The lab works out of the City Walk campus in Dubai, and its research is externally funded through the Dubai Research, Development and Innovation Grant Programme.

Research

Research directions

Our work covers the full path from raw electron microscopy sections to segmented organelles, and from clinical data to early diagnosis. We publish the code for every stage.

All research
vEM

Volume electron microscopy

Tile stitching, restoration, isotropic reconstruction and foundation-model instance segmentation, joined into one benchmarked pipeline. Anchored by the Dubai RDI grant "Towards high-resolution 3D reconstruction in volume electron microscopy".

FM

Foundation models for microscopy

Parameter-efficient adaptation of segment-anything style models to electron microscopy: low-rank adapters, layer-wise learning rate decay, and generalist versus specialist comparisons across datasets.

MED

Medical AI and clinical imaging

Neuroimaging and dementia biomarkers, prediction models built together with clinicians, and efficient architectures small enough to deploy on hospital and edge hardware.

MICROSAM

Microsam_llrd

LoRA and layer-wise learning rate decay fine-tuning of MicroSAM for mitochondria instance segmentation, trained and evaluated on Lucchi, UroCell, Kasthuri and VNC. Led at CIMA Lab.

Lead: CIMA Lab, Canadian University DubaiDirection: Foundation models for microscopy

DEMENTIA

Prodromal and preclinical markers for dementia

A Dubai RDI funded programme on identifying prodromal and preclinical markers for predicting diagnosis and progression in dementia, with models whose reasoning clinicians can inspect.

Principal Investigator: Dr. Firuz KamalovFunder: Dubai RDI Grant Programme
Grant page on Dubai RDI
vEMINR

vEMINR

Isotropic reconstruction for volume electron microscopy with implicit neural representations, published in Advanced Science 2026. A partner method from Prof. Renmin Han's group at Shandong University, mirrored in the CIMA Lab organisation.
